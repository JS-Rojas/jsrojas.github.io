Me!

I am a PhD researcher at OIST (Japan), with a scientific background shaped by Yachay Tech (Ecuador) and ICTP (Italy).

My research is driven by simple questions with surprising answers:

What makes a mathematical model a proper explanation of biological reality? Why do rare events dominate the behavior of complex systems? Can we build models and mechanisms from information theory principles?

Using deterministic and probabilistic models, I explore these questions. Here, I share my contributions:
